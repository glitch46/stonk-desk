    THE FINE PRINT, IN NORMAL-SIZED TYPE

    Know your numbers.

    RAW DATA ↗

    One token. Different lenses.

    Price comes from the deepest returned DexScreener pool. Supply-adjusted value uses that price × StonkFun's current total supply; it is not a verified circulating market cap. DEX volume and liquidity sum unique returned pools, not the whole market. Routed trades can touch multiple pools.

    Supply differences between providers will be shown here.

    Revenue is not your dividend.

    Revenue and buyback figures are reported by StonkFun. Revenue includes STONK-quoted launch fees, excludes STONK's own-pool fees, and is not profit. Direct STONK-fee burns require no open-market buy. The separate ecosystem flywheel is not counted as STONK buybacks.

    Ledger pricing coverage will appear here.

    Whales are addresses, not identities.

    Holder balances come from RugCheck's largest-account sample, using raw balances and that report's supply. Accounts are grouped by owner; known pool vaults are separated. Unmatched owners may still be custodians or contracts. An observed balance change is not proof of a buy or sale.

    ~24h deltas require two observations 20–28 hours apart, with the owner present in both. Provider cache age is unknown; collection time is shown below.
